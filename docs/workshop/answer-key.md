Use this to assess the [worksheet](participant-worksheet.md), not as a substitute
for evidence. Accept different wording when the subject and reasoning are correct.
Each answer needs a specific current-session or historical reference. The
[evidence index](../tutorial/evidence.md#evidence-by-decision) contains historical
examples; actual new-run identities and measurements must come from that run.

The historical F 5% window had 41 eligible treatments, ranked `workspace` p95
1,036.932 ms, and threshold 500 ms. Both aggregate and subgroup gates failed.
Do not require new-run measurements or treatment counts to equal those historical
numbers. Require the actual policy, cohort coverage and measured failure instead.

The separate aggregate-masking automated test supports why subgroup checks matter;
it is not the hosted observation. The unexplained off-state 502 is also separate
from the seeded delay. G's source repair makes no claim about its cause.

For transfer questions: disabling a flag prevents subsequent flagged behavior;
completed writes, migrations and in-flight effects need explicit recovery design.
Changed deployment/configuration requires compatible fresh evidence and a new
matching request/authorization, not reuse of an old approval.

Mark each checkpoint **supported**, **incomplete**, or **contradicted**, with its
reference and correction. These labels describe the submitted task response.
Do not infer durable learning, adoption, or revenue from this exercise.
